# FB ERP — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 November 2026_

FB ERP is an internal workforce app used by Faizan Brothers (Latifabad, Hyderabad, Pakistan) for staff attendance, sales targets and daily field reports.

## What the app stores

- Employee name, category, branch, login code and an optional profile photo.
- Attendance times (check in / check out).
- Sales targets, daily form entries (area names, shop counts, cartons sold) and badges.
- Location (GPS) at the moment of check in, check out and daily form save. It is used only to confirm the employee is within 100 metres of the office and to place sales areas on a map.

## Where the data is kept

All data is stored **only on the device** where the app is installed. The app does not send this data to our servers or to any third party. There are no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

Data leaves the device only when the user chooses to:

- share a report, report card image or backup file (for example to WhatsApp or Google Drive), or
- check for an app update (the app downloads a version number and the update file from GitHub; no personal data is sent).

Map tiles in the Area Heatmap are loaded from OpenStreetMap; only map tile requests are made, no personal data is sent.

## Permissions

- **Location** — to check employees in and out within 100 metres of the office, and to place areas on the map. Location is used only while the app is open and only when the employee taps a button.
- **Internet** — for app updates and map tiles.
- **Photos / files** — only when the user chooses a profile photo or a backup file.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all data from the device. An administrator can also delete any employee and their records inside the app.

## Children

The app is for company staff only and is not directed at children.
